# 🏛️ DOMAIN_COMPENDIUM.md — Example 2: Biotech & Longevity

## Layer 1: Philosophy & Epistemology (30%)
* **Antifragility (Nassim Nicholas Taleb):** Systems that gain from moderate disorder. Biological hormesis vs destructive chronic stress.
* **Via Negativa (Subtractive Medicine):** Health is improved faster by removing toxic stressors than by adding exotic supplements.

## Layer 2: Behavioral Psychology & Habits (30%)
* **Loss Aversion & Restriction Cycles:** How extreme deprivation triggers binge-compensation mechanisms in the basal ganglia.
* **Identity-Based Habits (James Clear):** Shifting focus from metric fixation (scale weight) to systemic self-perception.

## Layer 3: Endocrinology & Hard Biochemistry (20%)
* **Thyroid T3 Downregulation:** How severe caloric deficit signals starvation and lowers metabolic baseline rate.
* **Cortisol / Insulin Cross-Talk:** Why elevated stress hormones block lipolysis regardless of gym effort.
* **Circadian Photobiology:** Blue light exposure disrupting melatonin and deep sleep phases.

## Layer 4: Applied Biomarker Protocol (20%)
* **The 4-Panel Baseline:** Fasting glucose, HbA1c, hs-CRP, and Free T3 before starting any training intervention.
