# 🏛️ DOMAIN_COMPENDIUM.md — Example 1: Systems Researcher

## Layer 1: Philosophy & First Principles (30%)
* **Dichotomy of Control (Epictetus):** Suffering comes from trying to control outside outcomes rather than internal decisions.
* **The Observer Mindset (Eastern Non-Duality / Krishnamurti):** Consciousness is the observer registering thoughts, not thoughts themselves.
* **Non-Judgment as Mental Hygiene:** Self-criticism is an internal loop that burns cognitive energy without producing action.

## Layer 2: Psychology & Cognitive Mechanics (30%)
* **The Shadow (Carl Jung):** Disowned flaws projected onto others.
* **Internal Family Systems (IFS, Richard Schwartz):** The internal orchestra (The Judge, The Perfectionist, The Rebel) run by the core Self.
* **Cognitive Loops:** Over-analysis loops (Ti-Si traps) paralyzing action.

## Layer 3: Biology & Somatic Systems (20%)
* **Ultradian Rhythms:** Biological 90-minute wave cycles of human focus.
* **Allostatic Load:** The physiological cost of chronic stress and guilt.
* **Dopamine Baseline:** How cheap dopamine causes apathy and procrastination.

## Layer 4: Applied Framework & Digitization (20%)
* **Psychomatrix & Energy Diagnostics:** Calculating ratio between idea generation (Ones) and somatic stamina (Twos).
* **Sprint Protocol:** Strict time-boxed blocks calibrated to individual baseline capacity.
