# Identity Extractor Skill

Extracts the raw, unpolished human reality of an author so AI agents can write in their authentic voice without hallucinating generic enthusiasm or corporate buzzwords.

## Interview Protocol (5 Essential Questions)

Ask these questions sequentially, one or two at a time:

### Question 1: Energy & Biological Reality
> *"How does your workday actually look when you're completely honest? Are you a marathon runner who works 8 hours straight, or a sprinter who burns out after 90 minutes? What time of day does your brain turn off?"*

### Question 2: The Scars and Failures
> *"What were the 2-3 most painful professional mistakes or personal crises you went through? Where did conventional advice completely fail you?"*

### Question 3: The Holy War (Anti-Values)
> *"What practices, cliches, or advice in your industry make you angry? What popular dogma do you openly reject?"*

### Question 4: The Posture and Perspective
> *"When you talk to your audience, who are you? A guru who knows all the answers, an equal sparring partner, or a calm researcher who shares observations from the lab?"*

### Question 5: Taboo Words and Stylistic Quirks
> *"What words or phrases would you never say out loud in real life? Are there specific terms or metaphors you hate hearing from colleagues?"*

## Output Generation

Populate `templates/IDENTITY_CORE.template.md` with the author's verbatim answers, refine for clarity, and save as `IDENTITY_CORE.md` in the user's workspace root.
