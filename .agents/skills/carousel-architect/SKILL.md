# Carousel Architect Skill

This skill formats complex knowledge into visual, slide-by-slide progression designed for maximal Saves & Shares.

## Core Rules for 1080×1350 Carousels

1. **Cover Slide Domination (Slide 1):**
   * Acts as a billboard in the Instagram explore grid.
   * Large, bold typography (minimum 64pt).
   * High contrast (e.g. #0C0A0B dark canvas with white/cream text and subtle accent badge).
   * Include pagination (`01 / 10`) to prompt swiping.

2. **Information Density Control (Zero Wall-of-Text):**
   * Maximum 35–45 words per slide.
   * One core idea per slide.
   * Use visual rhythm: alternate between narrative text slides, comparison tables, and punchy single-sentence takeaway slides.

3. **The Save-Trigger Anatomy (Slide 10):**
   * Carousels rank by Save/Share ratio.
   * Slide 10 must summarize the protocol into an actionable 3-point checklist.
   * Explicit call to save: *"Сохраните пост, чтобы перестроить график на выходных"*.

## Procedure

1. Read `DOMAIN_COMPENDIUM.md` to select 1 mechanism.
2. Outline 8–10 slides following [templates/CAROUSEL.template.md](../../templates/CAROUSEL.template.md).
3. Ensure text passes `tools/lint_content.py` to eliminate AI fluff and pompous buzzwords.
